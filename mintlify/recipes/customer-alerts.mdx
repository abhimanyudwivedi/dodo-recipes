## What you'll have at the end

```
New customer: Ana Diaz from US, $50.00/month.
Ana Diaz changed plan to $99.00/month.
Payment failed for Ana Diaz. Worth a personal note today, before they drift.
Ana Diaz cancelled. Reason they gave: "too_expensive: Only need it in tax season"
Ana Diaz is back on $50.00/month.
```

Run `npm run preview` to print these from sample events. A new customer also gets a welcome email
at once, a "did you get your first result?" email on day 2, and one question on day 6. The sequence
stops by itself if they cancel.

## Who it's for, and what it replaces

Founders with their first paying customers, when every one of them matters and you want to hear
about them before your dashboard does. It replaces checking the dashboard every morning, a paid
onboarding-email tool, and finding out weeks later that someone left without anyone asking why.

## System design

```
Dodo webhook ─▶ api/dodo-webhook.js
                 verify signature (Standard Webhooks) on the raw body
                 seen this webhook id before? ─▶ stop (Dodo retries deliveries)
                 classify the event:
                   first subscription.active ─▶ "New customer" + schedule onboarding
                   active again later        ─▶ "is back"
                   on_hold / past_due / failed ─▶ "Payment failed"
                   cancelled                 ─▶ alert with their reason + stop onboarding
                   renewed                   ─▶ nothing (good news needs no ping)
                 ─▶ Slack / Discord / Telegram

Vercel cron, daily ─▶ api/send-onboarding.js ─▶ due steps, skip anyone who left ─▶ Resend
```

| Part | Where it runs | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Tables | Neon or Supabase Postgres | free tier |
| Webhook + daily cron | Vercel functions | free tier |
| Alerts | Slack / Discord incoming webhook, or a Telegram bot | free |
| Onboarding email | Resend | free up to 3,000 a month |

## Build it with your AI



<Steps>
  <Step title="Tables">
    "Postgres tables for customers (email, name, Dodo customer and subscription ids, status, first_paid_at, cancelled_at, unsubscribed_at), handled webhook ids, and onboarding emails (one row per customer per step, unique on both)."

    **Produces:** `schema.sql`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classifier">
    "A pure function that takes a Dodo subscription webhook and the customer row we already have, and returns: what happened, the alert text (or none), and whether to start or stop onboarding. First subscription.active is new, a later one is a return, renewals are quiet, cancellations quote cancellation_feedback and cancellation_comment. Tests for each."

    **Produces:** `lib/events.mjs`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Money">
    "Format Dodo's recurring_pre_tax_amount (smallest currency unit), currency and payment_frequency_interval as '$50.00/month'."

    **Produces:** `money()`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Webhook">
    "A Vercel function: verify the Standard Webhooks signature on the raw body, insert the webhook-id and stop if it existed, upsert the customer, send the alert, schedule or cancel onboarding."

    **Produces:** `api/dodo-webhook.js`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alerts">
    "Post a message to whichever of Slack, Discord and Telegram are configured; one failing must not block the others."

    **Produces:** `lib/notify.mjs`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarding">
    "Three short emails from the founder at day 0, 2 and 6, with placeholders; a daily cron sends what is due and skips anyone cancelled or unsubscribed."

    **Produces:** `lib/onboarding.mjs`, `api/send-onboarding.js`
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Run it

```
npm install && npm test
npm run preview
cp .env.example .env
psql "$DATABASE_URL" -f schema.sql
vercel deploy --prod
```

In the Dodo dashboard: Developer → Webhooks → add `https://<your-app>.vercel.app/api/dodo-webhook`
for the `subscription.*` events, and put the signing key in `DODO_WEBHOOK_KEY`. Use **test mode**
first: buy your own product with a test card and watch the alert arrive.

## Make it safe



<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Handle each webhook once">
    Dodo retries a delivery if your endpoint is slow or down, so the same event can arrive twice. The webhook id goes into a table first; a repeat stops there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A returning customer is not a new one">
    Only the first `subscription.active` per customer starts onboarding, or a customer who pauses and comes back gets "Welcome!" again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stop emailing people who left">
    A cancellation marks their queued emails as skipped, and the daily send checks again before every email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Verify the signature on the raw body">
    , before parsing it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Replies must reach a person">
    Onboarding emails set `reply_to` to your own inbox: an email that asks a question from a no-reply address is worse than none.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check your own payload">
    Field names here follow Dodo's API reference; open the Event catalog in your dashboard and compare one real test event before going live.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Charge for it with Dodo

<Tip>
**Every recipe ends here.** Dodo is the merchant of record, so tax and payment methods abroad are handled from the first sale.
</Tip>

This recipe is built on Dodo's subscription events, so it starts working the moment you sell with
[Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/?utm_source=recipes&utm_medium=gallery&utm_campaign=recipe-customer-alerts):

- **Collect the reason at the source.** Dodo's subscription carries `cancellation_feedback` and
  `cancellation_comment`, so the cancellation reason reaches you in the alert, not in a survey nobody
  fills in.
- **Act on failed payments.** `subscription.on_hold` and `subscription.past_due` arrive while there
  is still time to help: a personal note often saves the customer before the subscription ends.
- **Upgrades are signal too.** `subscription.plan_changed` tells you who is getting value. Those
  are the customers to ask for a quote or a referral.

## Built and used at Zipply

Zipply's own Dodo webhook already starts each new customer's first job search the moment they pay,
and its founder gets a phone notification for every payment. What it lacked was the other half: a
customer cancelled and nobody knew why until days later. This recipe is that missing half, written
so any founder on Dodo can have both from day one.
