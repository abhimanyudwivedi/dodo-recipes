Every recipe has the same seven parts

1 What you'll have at the end 2 Who it's for, what it replaces 3 System design: parts, where, cost 4 Build it with your AI: the prompts 5 Make it safe: the checks in code 6 Charge for it with Dodo 7 How it's used on a real business

Part 5 is the one generic templates leave out. It's the list of things that went wrong when the tool ran for real: a mail search that matched every sender, a daily job that reported success while skipping customers, a number in an ad that drifted from the source. Each one is now a check in code, with a test.

Part 6 is why these tools exist. A cold email that converts needs a payment link; an ad needs a checkout that tells you which hook sold; a report is worth sending only to customers who pay.
