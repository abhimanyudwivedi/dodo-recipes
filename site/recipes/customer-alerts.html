Know the minute someone pays, and why they leave

A Dodo Payments webhook that pings you in Slack, Discord or Telegram when someone buys, upgrades, fails a payment or cancels, with the cancellation reason they typed. It also starts and stops a short onboarding email sequence on its own.

Run npm run preview to print these from sample events. A new customer also gets a welcome email at once, a "did you get your first result?" email on day 2, and one question on day 6. The sequence stops by itself if they cancel.

02Who it's for, and what it replaces

Founders with their first paying customers, when every one of them matters and you want to hear about them before your dashboard does. It replaces checking the dashboard every morning, a paid onboarding-email tool, and finding out weeks later that someone left without anyone asking why.

03System design

Dodo webhook ─▶ api/dodo-webhook.js
                 verify signature (Standard Webhooks) on the raw body
                 seen this webhook id before? ─▶ stop (Dodo retries deliveries)
                 classify the event:
                   first subscription.active ─▶ "New customer" + schedule onboarding
                   active again later        ─▶ "is back"
                   on_hold / past_due / failed ─▶ "Payment failed"
                   cancelled                 ─▶ alert with their reason + stop onboarding
                   renewed                   ─▶ nothing (good news needs no ping)
                 ─▶ Slack / Discord / Telegram

Vercel cron, daily ─▶ api/send-onboarding.js ─▶ due steps, skip anyone who left ─▶ Resend

04Build it with your AI

  1. Tables. "Postgres tables for customers (email, name, Dodo customer and subscription ids, status, first_paid_at, cancelled_at, unsubscribed_at), handled webhook ids, and onboarding emails (one row per customer per step, unique on both)." → schema.sql
  2. Classifier. "A pure function that takes a Dodo subscription webhook and the customer row we already have, and returns: what happened, the alert text (or none), and whether to start or stop onboarding. First subscription.active is new, a later one is a return, renewals are quiet, cancellations quote cancellation_feedback and cancellation_comment. Tests for each." → lib/events.mjs
  3. Money. "Format Dodo's recurring_pre_tax_amount (smallest currency unit), currency and payment_frequency_interval as '$50.00/month'." → money()
  4. Webhook. "A Vercel function: verify the Standard Webhooks signature on the raw body, insert the webhook-id and stop if it existed, upsert the customer, send the alert, schedule or cancel onboarding." → api/dodo-webhook.js
  5. Alerts. "Post a message to whichever of Slack, Discord and Telegram are configured; one failing must not block the others." → lib/notify.mjs
  6. Onboarding. "Three short emails from the founder at day 0, 2 and 6, with placeholders; a daily cron sends what is due and skips anyone cancelled or unsubscribed." → lib/onboarding.mjs, api/send-onboarding.js

05Make it safe

  • Handle each webhook once. Dodo retries a delivery if your endpoint is slow or down, so the same event can arrive twice. The webhook id goes into a table first; a repeat stops there.
  • A returning customer is not a new one. Only the first subscription.active per customer starts onboarding, or a customer who pauses and comes back gets "Welcome!" again.
  • Stop emailing people who left. A cancellation marks their queued emails as skipped, and the daily send checks again before every email.
  • Verify the signature on the raw body, before parsing it.
  • Replies must reach a person. Onboarding emails set reply_to to your own inbox: an email that asks a question from a no-reply address is worse than none.
  • Check your own payload. Field names here follow Dodo's API reference; open the Event catalog in your dashboard and compare one real test event before going live.

06Charge for it with Dodo

This recipe is built on Dodo's subscription events, so it starts working the moment you sell with Dodo Payments:

  • Collect the reason at the source. Dodo's subscription carries cancellation_feedback and cancellation_comment, so the cancellation reason reaches you in the alert, not in a survey nobody fills in.
  • Act on failed payments. subscription.on_hold and subscription.past_due arrive while there is still time to help: a personal note often saves the customer before the subscription ends.
  • Upgrades are signal too. subscription.plan_changed tells you who is getting value. Those are the customers to ask for a quote or a referral.

07Built and used at Zipply

Zipply's own Dodo webhook already starts each new customer's first job search the moment they pay, and its founder gets a phone notification for every payment. What it lacked was the other half: a customer cancelled and nobody knew why until days later. This recipe is that missing half, written so any founder on Dodo can have both from day one.
